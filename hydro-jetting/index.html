Hydro jetting for drains and sewer lines

Emergency Hydro Jetting in Oak Ridge, TN

A cable pokes a hole through a clog. A hydro jet scours the whole pipe wall. When your drain keeps clogging or a main line backs up, jetting can clear what the snake leaves behind.

Hydro jetting pushes high-pressure water through a nozzle that scours the inside of a drain or sewer line. It cuts roots, strips grease and breaks down scale that a cable cannot touch. Our crews offer it in Oak Ridge, TN, including after-hours calls when a main line has backed up.

When a sewer backup calls for jetting

A single clogged fixture does not need a jet. A main line that backs up into the lowest drain does, especially if cable work has failed or the clog has come back. If sewage is coming into the house, stop using water, keep people and pets away from the wet area and call us any hour.

A backup is an emergency because the water carries bacteria and spreads fast. Same-day and after-hours visits are available, and the dispatcher will tell you what we know about timing when you call.

We often start with a cable to open the line, then use the jet to finish the job. For comparison, see our drain cleaning page.

The camera comes first

Jetting is the wrong tool for a collapsed or badly cracked pipe. Water pressure can make damage worse, and no cleaning fixes a broken line. We run a camera through the pipe before jetting so we can see roots, grease, sags and cracks. The sewer inspection page explains what that step shows.

If the camera finds a break, we explain the repair options and the cost. If the pipe is sound, jetting restores the line to nearly full diameter.

Roots, grease and scale: what each one needs

Roots enter through joints and thicken into a mat. A jet with a cutting nozzle shreds the mat, and the water flushes the pieces away. Grease needs hot water, or a nozzle that strips the film from the wall. Scale takes a rotating head. We choose the nozzle to fit the pipe.

Why homes in older neighborhoods see more root trouble

Older lines have more joints and more years of settling. Mature trees on a lot send roots toward the moisture in those joints. A jet clears the roots, but if the joint stays broken they will return. In that case a sewer line repair is the lasting answer.

How a jetting visit runs

  1. We find the access point, usually a cleanout, and set the equipment.
  2. The camera confirms the pipe is sound enough to jet.
  3. The jet runs the line in passes, working back toward the access point.
  4. A final camera run shows the result so you see the pipe yourself.
  5. You get the findings and a plan to keep the line open.

Pricing

The price depends on the length of line, the access, the severity of the buildup and the time of day. Jetting costs more than a cable visit because of the equipment. We state the price before we start and tell you if a cheaper method will do the job.

Jetting follows the pipe maker's pressure guidelines and the local plumbing code, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC) or the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). That is why we run a camera first. Sound pipe handles the pressure, and damaged pipe does not.
