Hidden leak detection

Emergency Leak Detection in Oak Ridge, TN

Some leaks announce themselves. Others hide in a wall, under a slab or in a yard line and show up only on the water bill. We find the source before anyone cuts into anything.

A hidden leak wastes water and damages framing, subfloors and ceilings while you cannot see it. Our leak detection service in Oak Ridge, TN is built around one idea: find the exact spot first, then open the smallest possible hole.

When a hidden leak is an emergency

  • A water bill that jumps with no change in how you live.
  • A water meter that keeps moving when every fixture is off.
  • Warm spots on the floor, a sign of a leak in a hot water line.
  • Musty smells, peeling paint or stains that keep returning.
  • The sound of running water when nothing is on.
  • Soft, green or unusually wet patches in the yard between the meter and the house.

If water is actively spraying, skip this page and see burst pipe repair. If the signs above fit, a quick meter test, which you can start yourself, tells you whether water is leaving the system.

A simple meter test you can do now

  • Turn off every fixture and appliance that uses water.
  • Write down the meter reading, or note the position of the small leak indicator.
  • Wait 30 minutes without using any water.
  • Check the meter again. Movement means a leak between the meter and your fixtures.

How we find the leak

We start by narrowing the zone. Pressure tests isolate the hot side from the cold side and the house from the yard line. Then electronic listening gear picks up the sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe through floors and walls.

Slab leaks and older Oak Ridge homes

Many Oak Ridge homes sit on slabs, and the supply lines run beneath them. Over decades, a line can wear through at a bend or rub against gravel. The signs are a hot spot on the floor, a drop in pressure or the sound of water at night. Locating the spot before cutting the slab saves a great deal of mess.

Older homes may also carry galvanized lines that corrode from the inside and leak at several places. When a line has more than one leak, repair after repair costs more than a repipe, and we will explain the numbers rather than patch endlessly.

Yard lines and the meter

The line from the meter to the house belongs to the homeowner, and the Oak Ridge utility handles the meter and its side of the connection. If we find a leak on the yard line, we repair or replace the run. If the leak is at the meter, we will tell you to contact the City of Oak Ridge at (865) 425-1600.

After we find it

  1. We mark the leak location and explain what we found.
  2. You get the repair options and the price before any wall or floor is opened.
  3. We repair the pipe, test it and restore the access point we made.
  4. You get the notes and photos for your insurer.

Insurance often covers sudden damage and may handle the cost of finding a leak, but each policy is different. Ask your agent before the visit, and keep the written findings.

What the price depends on

The price depends on the size of the zone, the access and the methods we need. A simple pressure test costs less than a full survey with listening gear and camera work. We quote the visit before we begin.

Pressure testing follows the local plumbing code, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC) or the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). We test at pressures suited to the pipe material, because a test that stresses old pipe can create a leak instead of finding one.
