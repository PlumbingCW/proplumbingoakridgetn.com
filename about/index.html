Who we are

About Oak Ridge Pro Plumbing

We are a plumbing service in Oak Ridge, TN, built around one dependable thing: a dispatch line that answers day or night, and a plumber who explains the price before work starts.

What we do

Oak Ridge Pro Plumbing serves homes and businesses in Oak Ridge, Clinton, Oliver Springs and Norris, Tennessee. We handle drains, sewer lines, water heaters, leaks, burst pipes, fixtures and remodel plumbing, and we answer emergency calls any hour of the day.

We do not list awards, years in business or review counts on this site, because we only publish what we can show. What we can tell you is how we work, and that is on this page.

How we work

  1. A real person answers the phone and asks what is happening.
  2. You get safe first steps right away, such as where to shut off the water.
  3. The plumber finds the cause and explains the options in plain words.
  4. You hear the price before work begins, and the plumber stops to talk if the job changes.
  5. We test the work and show you what to watch afterward.

Why we say when something can wait

A dripping faucet is not an emergency, and we would rather tell you so than send a truck at midnight. A burst pipe or a sewage backup is. Part of our job is helping you tell the difference, and the dispatcher will do that honestly on the call.

Licensed and insured

Our plumbers work as a licensed and insured company. If you want to check any plumber's license in Tennessee, the State of Tennessee offers a contractor license search at search.cloud.commerce.tn.gov, or you can call 1-800-544-7693.

What a first call sounds like

The dispatcher begins with your address and the problem, then asks a few questions. Is water still flowing? Can you reach the shutoff? Is anyone in danger? Those answers decide how fast we move and what you do while you wait. You do not need to know plumbing words. Describe what you see, hear or smell, and we will translate.

Once we confirm the visit, we tell you what we know about timing. Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows, and we say so when it does not, because a promise we cannot keep is worse than a slower honest answer.

What we will not do

  • Push a replacement when a repair would hold.
  • Start work you have not approved.
  • Hide a change in price until the invoice.
  • Claim a number of years, jobs or reviews we cannot show.
  • Leave you without safe first steps while you wait.

Why older houses shape how we work

Oak Ridge began in 1942 as a Manhattan Project town and grew fast, so many homes carry pipe from several eras and remodels by several owners. We do not guess from the street. A camera look, a pressure test or an open access panel tells us what is actually in the line, and the repair follows from that.

It also shapes the advice we give. A mid-century house with galvanized supply lines needs a different conversation than a newer home with plastic pipe, and we tailor the options to what we find.

Homes, rentals and businesses

The same line serves a homeowner with a leaking heater, a landlord with a tenant's backed-up drain and a shop owner whose restroom has closed. Each has different stakes. A landlord needs notes and photos for the file, and a business needs the fastest path back to open doors. Tell the dispatcher which you are, and we plan the visit around it.

For larger or multi-unit buildings, see our commercial plumbing page. For work you can plan, such as a water heater swap or a remodel, we schedule a daytime visit and walk the job with you first.
