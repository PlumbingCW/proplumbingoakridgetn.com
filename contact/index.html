What to have ready when you call

  1. The street address of the property.
  2. What you see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  3. Whether you can reach the main shutoff and whether you have turned it off.
  4. Whether the building is on city sewer or septic.
  5. A callback number.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or your gas utility first, then call us for repairs.
