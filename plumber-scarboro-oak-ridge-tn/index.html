Scarboro | Oak Ridge, Anderson County

Plumber in Scarboro, Oak Ridge, TN

Scarboro is a historic residential community in the Gamble Valley area of Oak Ridge, opened in 1950. Many of its homes began as duplexes, so we pay attention to how a house has been changed over the years.

About the Scarboro community

During the Manhattan Project, housing in Oak Ridge was segregated by race. Black workers and their families lived in crowded one-room hutments, and the area where Scarboro now stands was first a trailer camp served by the Gamble Valley school. Scarboro Village opened in 1950 as a permanent community built to the architects' 1948 master plan.

The Park Service records that Scarboro schools were part of the story of Oak Ridge becoming one of the first Southern school systems to integrate, in 1955. A monument to the Scarboro 85 was added in 2023.

What the houses are like, according to the survey

A National Park Service historic district form describes Scarboro as dominated by duplexes, with frame two-family models of about 576 square feet per unit, along with some concrete block single-family houses. The form says the most common change in Scarboro is the conversion of duplexes into single-family homes.

It also says Scarboro has seen more real estate development than neighboring Woodland, and that its original commercial area was lost to a fire and replaced by a modern building. The Scarboro Community Center, run by Oak Ridge Recreation and Parks, is a landmark on Hampton Road.

Why conversions matter to a plumber

When a duplex becomes one home, water lines, drains, a second kitchen or a second water heater may have been combined, capped or moved. We have no record of what was done in any particular house. The plumber starts by tracing what is actually there, and we tell you if a camera look would help before we quote.

The same goes for the line to the street. If the problem is on the public side, the City of Oak Ridge utility office at (865) 425-3400 can tell you where its responsibility starts.
