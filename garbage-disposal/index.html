Garbage disposal service

Emergency Garbage Disposal Repair in Oak Ridge, TN

A disposal that hums but will not spin, leaks into the cabinet or backs up the sink is a daily nuisance. We free jams, fix leaks and replace units that are past saving.

A garbage disposal looks simple, which is why people are surprised when it fails. A jammed motor, a worn seal or a drain line that clogs behind it can each look the same from above the sink. We sort out which it is.

Disposal emergency: safety first

Never put a hand or a tool other than a broom handle into a disposal. Switch the power off at the wall switch and, for any work, at the breaker. A disposal can start with force if someone flips the switch while a hand is inside.

A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the housing has cracked or a seal has failed inside. Those units are replaced, not repaired.

What a disposal can and cannot take

  • Small soft food scraps, run with cold water.
  • No grease, oil or fat, which hardens down the line.
  • No coffee grounds in quantity, which pack into sludge.
  • No fibrous items such as celery, corn husks or potato peels in volume.
  • No bones, fruit pits or hard shells.
  • No glass, metal, plastic or cleaning chemicals.

Replacing the unit

If the motor has failed or the housing leaks, replacement is the answer. We match the horsepower to how you use it, check the sink flange and rewire or reconnect the power. A disposal is wired to a switch or an outlet, and we check that the circuit and the dishwasher connection are correct.

Many kitchens with a dishwasher have a drain hose tied into the disposal. If the dishwasher backs up into the sink, a blocked disposal inlet or a missing knockout plug can be the cause, which we check on every visit.

Septic systems and disposals

Some properties around Oak Ridge use septic systems, and a disposal adds solids to a tank. If you have septic, tell us, because it changes what we advise about using one.

Care that makes a disposal last

  1. Run cold water before, during and for about 15 seconds after using it.
  2. Feed scraps in slowly rather than all at once.
  3. Grind a few ice cubes now and then to scour the chamber.
  4. Wipe the splash guard and clean it with soap and a brush.
  5. Use the reset button only after finding why it tripped.

What affects the price

Unjamming a unit costs less than replacing it. A replacement depends on the horsepower and any plumbing changes. We state the number first.

Repair or replace the disposal

A jam, a tripped reset or a loose gasket costs little to fix, and the unit often keeps running for years afterward. Replace it when the housing leaks, the motor will not start after a reset or the bearings have worn from repeated jams. If it is old and failing, a repair visit can approach the price of a new unit, and we will tell you so plainly.

Before we replace one, we check the sink flange, the drain trap and the dishwasher connection, since a new disposal on a worn trap will leak again.

Wiring follows the National Electrical Code (NEC), NFPA 70, as your jurisdiction has adopted it, and the drain connection follows the local plumbing code, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC) or the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Ask the local office whether your work needs a permit.
