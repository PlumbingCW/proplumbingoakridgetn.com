Faucet repair

Emergency Faucet Repair in Oak Ridge, TN

A drip wastes water and a failed faucet wastes your morning. We fix leaks, low flow and stuck handles, and we replace faucets and the shutoff valves beneath them.

Most faucet trouble starts small. A slow drip, a handle that has gone stiff or a spray that goes sideways. Those small problems add up in your water bill and in the cabinet below, where a slow leak can rot the base for months before you notice.

What the drip is telling you

Faucet symptoms and causes
SymptomLikely causeRepair
Drip from the spoutWorn cartridge, washer or seatReplace the cartridge or washer and seat
Leak at the handle baseWorn O-ring or loose packing nutReplace the O-ring, tighten the packing
Low flowClogged aerator or scale in the valveClean or replace the aerator and the cartridge
Water under the sinkFailed supply line or shutoffReplace the line or the valve
Hard to turnMineral deposits, worn stemClean or replace the stem or cartridge
Wobbly spoutLoose mounting nutTighten and reseal

A faucet that drips once a second can waste thousands of gallons over a year. If you are not sure where the water comes from, a quick check under the sink with a dry paper towel often reveals whether the leak is in the faucet or the supply.

Repair or replace

Repair makes sense when the faucet is a decent model and the part is available. Replacement makes sense when the finish has worn, parts are discontinued or the fixture has leaked more than once. We tell you which one saves money.

If a leak is not at the faucet but in the wall, leak detection is the next step.

When a faucet leak is an emergency

Many homes have the original shutoff valves under the sink, and many will not close or will leak when moved. A valve that does not close turns a small repair into a main shutoff job. We replace failing stops as part of faucet work so the next repair is easy.

What a faucet replacement includes

  • Removing the old faucet and cleaning the mounting surface.
  • New supply lines and, where needed, new shutoff valves.
  • Mounting, sealing and aligning the new faucet.
  • Flushing the lines and cleaning the aerator before the first use.
  • A leak test under the sink before we leave.

Water quality and your fixtures

Mineral buildup clogs aerators and wears cartridges. We have not verified a hardness figure for Oak Ridge, so we suggest testing your tap or asking the City of Oak Ridge for its water quality report at (865) 425-1600. If scale is the reason a faucet fails every few years, that information shapes what you buy.

Kitchens, baths and outdoor faucets

Kitchen faucets get heavy use, and pull-down sprayers wear their hoses. Bathroom faucets get slow leaks that stain basins. Outdoor spigots can freeze and split inside the wall during cold snaps, which is a repair that starts in the spring when you turn the water on and see it in the basement. If an outdoor line is leaking inside, call us and see burst pipe repair.

What affects the price

A cartridge swap costs far less than a full replacement with new valves. The faucet you choose changes the price, and so does the access under the sink. We quote the visit before we begin.

Faucets and supply parts that touch drinking water should be certified to NSF/ANSI 61 and made to the ASME A112.18.1 standard for plumbing supply fittings. We install parts that carry those marks.
