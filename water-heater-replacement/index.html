Water heater replacement

Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Oak Ridge, TN

A tank that has failed will not come back, and a leaking one can flood a garage in an hour. We replace water heaters in Oak Ridge around the clock and explain your options before anything is removed.

When a tank water heater leaks from the shell, replacement is the only real fix. Our 24/7 line in Oak Ridge, TN takes emergency water heater replacement calls any hour, and we can often schedule the swap the same day when the schedule allows.

The first ten minutes: what to do while you wait

  1. Turn off the cold water supply valve on top of the heater.
  2. Switch off the power at the breaker for an electric unit, or set a gas unit's control to off.
  3. Open a hot tap upstairs to let pressure out, then leave it open.
  4. Place a bucket or wet vacuum under the tank and move stored items out of the water's path.
  5. Call us and tell us the tank size, the fuel type and where the heater sits.

The tank size and fuel type let us bring the right unit on the first trip. If you can read the label on the side of the tank, the model number helps us confirm the venting and gas or electrical needs.

Repair, replace or go tankless

A leak from a fitting, valve or element often can be fixed, and our water heater repair page covers those. A leak from the tank shell cannot. Corrosion has eaten through the steel, and no patch holds.

Age helps decide. Many manufacturers rate tank heaters for several years of service, and the warranty card or serial number will tell you the build date. A heater that is at the end of its life and failing for the second time in a year is usually cheaper to replace than to keep repairing.

Why Oak Ridge garages and closets matter

Many Oak Ridge homes were built with the heater in a closet, a carport utility room or a crawlspace, because the original plans were tight. A closet install limits the tank height and the clearance around the flue. We measure before we promise a model, and we flag venting problems so they do not surprise you at install.

If your heater sits in an attached garage, local code rules on elevation and ignition protection may apply. Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Oak Ridge building office whether your swap needs a permit, and we will tell you what the plumber can handle on site.

What you get on replacement day

  • The old tank drained, disconnected and hauled away.
  • New flex connectors, a new temperature and pressure relief valve and a discharge line.
  • A check of the water pressure and, where it applies, an expansion tank.
  • The gas line or electrical connection tested before the unit is lit.
  • A short walkthrough covering the thermostat setting and flushing schedule.

A thermostat setting of 120 degrees Fahrenheit is the Department of Energy's general recommendation for home use. We set it there unless you ask otherwise.

How the price is built

The price follows the tank size, fuel type, venting and how easy the old unit is to reach. Code upgrades, such as a new vent liner or a drain pan, add to it. We state the number up front and never start work on an upgrade without your yes.

Gas storage heaters are certified to ANSI Z21.10.1, and the relief valve on any tank follows ANSI Z21.22. Installation follows the manufacturer's instructions and the local code, which is based on the IPC or the UPC. We confirm the label on your tank and the clearances before we set the new unit.
