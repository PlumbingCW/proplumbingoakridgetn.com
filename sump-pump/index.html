Sump pump service

Emergency Sump Pump Repair in Oak Ridge, TN

A sump pump sits quietly until the rain comes and then decides whether your basement stays dry. When it fails, you need a fix fast. We repair, replace and back up sump pumps around the clock.

Parts of the Oak Ridge area sit among ridges and valleys, and rain runs downhill. Homes at the base of a slope or with finished basements often rely on a sump pump to move groundwater away from the foundation. If the pump stops during a storm, the water finds the lowest place in your house.

When a sump pump becomes an emergency

  • The pit is full and the pump is silent.
  • The motor hums but does not move water.
  • The pump runs constantly and the pit never empties.
  • Water is rising near the pit during heavy rain.
  • The power is out and there is no battery backup.

If water is rising, keep out of the area if outlets or appliances are submerged and switch off the power to that circuit from a dry spot. Call us and tell us what you see.

Pumps wear out, and a unit that has run for many years during wet seasons may be near the end of its life. A pump that runs only occasionally can last longer. We tell you the pump's likely remaining life when we see its condition.

Repair, replace or add a backup

A new float switch or check valve is a modest repair. A seized motor is usually a replacement. If your pump has failed once during a storm, a battery backup is worth considering, because storms often cut power at the same moment the pump is needed most.

Options worth discussing

A primary pump moves the water, and a backup pump on a battery takes over when power fails or the primary quits. Some homeowners also add a water-powered backup where the water pressure allows, and an alarm that sounds when the pit level rises.

The discharge matters too

A pump is only as good as where it sends the water. The discharge line should carry water well away from the foundation, and it should not freeze or clog in winter. We check the line, the check valve and the outlet, because a pump that discharges next to the house just returns the water to the pit.

Local rules limit where a sump discharge can go, and connecting it to the sanitary sewer is generally not allowed. Ask the City of Oak Ridge or your local office about the rules for your property, and we will install to what they require.

The service visit

  1. We test the pump and the float, and watch a full cycle.
  2. We check the check valve, the discharge line and the pit.
  3. You hear the findings and the options, with the price.
  4. We repair or replace the pump and test it again.
  5. Before we leave, you see the pump run, and we explain the maintenance.

What affects the price

The price follows the pump type, the discharge work, the backup system and whether the pit needs work. We quote the job first. For a sump pump that ties into other issues, such as a basement leak, see leak detection.

Sump pump work follows the manufacturer's guidelines and the local code. Electrical connections follow the National Electrical Code (NEC), which calls for ground fault protection on many receptacles in damp locations, so we check that the outlet and circuit suit the pump.
