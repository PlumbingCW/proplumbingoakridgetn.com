Water Heater Valve Replacement Cost: An Oak Ridge, TN Price Guide

The price of a valve replacement depends on which valve it is, how hard it is to reach and whether the tank is worth saving. We explain the factors and tell you why we quote after we look.

By the Oak Ridge Pro Plumbing team · Published November 16, 2026 · Updated November 16, 2026

People search for water heater valve replacement cost because a valve is dripping and they want to know whether it is a small job or a big one. The honest answer is that there are several valves, and the price depends on which one has failed and what surrounds it.

We do not publish a flat dollar figure for Oak Ridge. A number from a national website tells you little about your tank, your closet or your hour of the day. What we can do is show you what moves the price, so the quote you get makes sense.

The valves on a tank water heater

Which valve does what
ValveWhere it sitsJob
Cold water shutoffOn the cold pipe above the heaterStops water to the tank
Temperature and pressure relief valveSide or top of the tankReleases pressure or heat for safety
Drain valveLow on the tankDrains the tank for flushing or removal
Gas control valveAt the burner on gas heatersControls the burner and the pilot
Gas shutoffOn the gas line near the heaterStops gas to the heater

A failed drain valve or a shutoff is usually a straightforward repair. A relief valve is a safety part and also simple, as covered in our guide to replacing the pressure valve. A gas control valve is a more costly part and needs care.

What moves the price

Five factors

  • Which valve it is. A drain valve is not a gas control.
  • Access. A tank in an open garage is quicker than a tank in a tight closet.
  • The condition of the pipe around it. Corroded neighbors can snap.
  • Whether the tank must be drained first, and how long that takes.
  • The hour. After-hours dispatch costs more to staff than a weekday visit.

When a valve is hard to turn, the plumber often finds that the one that has failed is not the only one that is old. Replacing a shutoff valve that has seized can turn into a bigger job, because the pipe it sits on may need repair.

The cold water shutoff is the one that bites

The shutoff above a water heater is used rarely, and old gate valves often will not close or leak at the stem when turned. If the shutoff will not close, the plumber has to shut off water at the main to do any repair. Replacing a failed shutoff with a ball valve makes every future repair easier.

Valve repair versus replacing the heater

A new valve on an old tank is worthwhile if the tank is sound. If the tank is near the end of its life, you may pay for the repair and replace the heater in a year. The signs of an aging tank are rust at the base, rusty hot water, repeated leaks and a manufacture date many years back. The serial number on the label tells you the build date.

When a valve repair and a replacement look close in price, we tell you plainly which we would pick. Our water heater replacement page covers the numbers.

Questions that change the quote

  1. Is the heater gas or electric?
  2. Which valve is leaking, and where is the water coming from?
  3. How old is the heater, and has it been repaired before?
  4. Is the heater in a closet, a garage, an attic or a basement?
  5. Is the leak active, or is it a slow drip?

Answering these on the call lets us give you a better idea of the job before the visit. The final number comes after the plumber sees it.

What a fair quote looks like

A good quote names the part, the work and the conditions. It tells you what is included, what would add cost and when the plumber would stop to check with you. It does not include work you have not approved. If a quote is only a number with no detail, ask for more.

Relief valves for tank heaters meet ANSI Z21.22, gas storage heaters meet ANSI Z21.10.1, and installation follows the local plumbing code, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC) or the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).

Safety before anyone touches a valve

  • Turn off the electric breaker or set the gas control to off before any valve work.
  • Close the cold supply, and open a hot tap so the tank can drain safely.
  • Never cap or plug a relief valve discharge pipe.
  • If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 or your gas utility before anyone calls a plumber.
  • If the tank is hot to the touch and leaking, keep children and pets away.

Oak Ridge note

Permit rules for water heater work vary. Ask the City of Oak Ridge building office, or the office for your town, whether a valve replacement needs one. For a gas valve, ask your gas utility about requirements as well.

To check that a plumber is licensed in Tennessee, use the State contractor license search at search.cloud.commerce.tn.gov, or call 1-800-544-7693. The $25,000 threshold applies to contractor licensing for larger projects.
