Plumbing Service Area in and Around Oak Ridge, TN

We are based in Oak Ridge and dispatch around the clock to Clinton, Oliver Springs and Norris. Here is how each place fits and what to tell us when you call.

Oak Ridge, our home base

Our office is at 1300 Oak Ridge Turnpike, Oak Ridge, TN 37830, and the 24/7 dispatch line serves homes and businesses across the city. Oak Ridge spreads along ridge and valley, so a lot may sit on a hillside or on flat ground, and that affects drain slope and where a cleanout sits.

When you call from Oak Ridge, give the street and the nearest cross street if you can. Hillside streets can be hard to find at night, and the extra detail helps the plumber find you.

Clinton, the Anderson County seat

Clinton sits on the Clinch River north of Oak Ridge. We cover it with dispatch from Oak Ridge, and it has its own page with the six calls we hear most from the area. Read the emergency plumber page for Clinton for ZIP codes, first steps and the local detail.

Oliver Springs

Oliver Springs lies west of Oak Ridge across Roane, Anderson and Morgan counties. The town grew around the railroad and coal, with a depot built in 1896 and mining at Windrock from about 1904. Much of its older housing dates from that era, so older supply lines and drains are common, and we check the pipe before we quote.

The ZIP code is 37840. Call and give the street address so the dispatcher can confirm the visit.

Norris

Norris was planned by the Tennessee Valley Authority beginning in 1933, with a dozen house designs, and its historic district was listed on the National Register in 1975. Small, well-built houses of that type can hide old pipe behind plaster, so a leak there deserves a careful look before anyone cuts a wall.

Norris uses ZIP codes 37828 and 37705. Call with the street address and we will confirm coverage.

Hills, ridges and finding your house

Oak Ridge sits among five ridges named Black Oak, East Fork, Pine, Chestnut and Haw, with valleys between them. Streets curve around the slopes, and addresses can be hard to find in the dark. If you can, turn on a porch light, move a car from the curb and tell the dispatcher about any landmark such as a church, a school or a trailhead near your street.

City sewer or septic

Most homes in the city connect to the sewer, but some outlying properties around the area use septic systems. The two behave differently in a backup. A city sewer problem often shows in several fixtures at once and may involve the public main. A septic problem can mean a full tank or a failed drain field. Tell the dispatcher which you have, and the first steps change to fit.
