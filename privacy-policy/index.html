Privacy Policy

We keep this short. This site has no forms, and the main way to reach us is by phone. Here is what that means for your information.

Calls and call recording

Calls to our dispatch line may be recorded and transcribed for quality, training and to make sure we record your address and problem correctly. By staying on the call, you agree to this recording. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

What we collect

When you call, we may collect your phone number, the property address, the nature of the plumbing problem and what you say on the call. We use this to dispatch a plumber, give you a price, complete the work and keep a record of the visit.

This website does not have forms, accounts or checkout, and it does not ask you to submit personal information through a page.

Server logs and cookies

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which can include your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We use them to keep the site running and secure. We do not sell this information.

How we share information

We share information only with the people who need it to do the job, such as the plumber on your visit, and with service providers who help us run the phone line and the site. We may disclose information when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, subject to what we must keep for records and the law. Call (855) 575-2890 or email contact@proplumbingoakridgetn.com.

Children

This site is for adults who manage property and plumbing. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of the page. Questions can go to contact@proplumbingoakridgetn.com or to our office at 1300 Oak Ridge Turnpike, Oak Ridge, TN 37830.
