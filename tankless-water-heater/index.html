Tankless Water Heater in Oak Ridge, TN

Tankless heaters heat water as you use it, so the hot water does not run out. They also depend on the right gas line, the right vent and regular descaling. We handle all three.

A tankless water heater in Oak Ridge, TN delivers hot water on demand and takes less floor space than a tank. It is not a bolt-on swap, though. The unit needs a gas line sized for its load, a vent rated for its exhaust and water that does not scale it up in a few years.

Is tankless right for your house?

The best fits are households that run out of hot water with a tank, homes where closet space is tight and owners who plan to stay long enough to recoup the higher up-front cost. The worst fits are homes where the gas line cannot be upsized economically.

Questions that decide tankless
QuestionWhy it matters
How big is the gas line to the heater?Most tankless units draw far more gas at peak than a tank burner.
Where can the vent go?Tankless exhaust needs a rated vent path, often through a wall or the roof.
How hard is your water?Scale builds inside the heat exchanger. Test your tap or ask the utility.
How many fixtures run at once?Flow rate limits how many showers one unit can serve.
Do you want a recirculation loop?It shortens the wait for hot water but adds parts.

Installation, done in the right order

We start by measuring the gas load and the existing line. If the line is too small, we tell you the options, and the price, before any work begins. Then we plan the vent, the condensate drain on high-efficiency units and the electrical outlet the unit needs.

Isolation valves go in on both water lines so the unit can be flushed later. Without them, descaling is harder, which is why we add them on every install. If you are comparing against a tank, see water heater installation.

Descaling and annual service

Hard water leaves scale on the heat exchanger, and scale cuts efficiency and triggers error codes. A flush with a pump and a descaling solution, run through the isolation valves, clears it. How often depends on your water and the manufacturer's schedule.

We have not verified a water hardness figure for Oak Ridge, so we advise testing the tap or asking the City of Oak Ridge for its water quality report at (865) 425-1600. The result tells you whether a yearly flush is wise or a water softener is worth a look.

Signs your tankless unit needs service

  • Error codes that return after a reset.
  • Hot water that cycles hot and cold.
  • A drop in flow at the hot taps.
  • Rumbling or popping from the unit.
  • A yearly flush overdue by the manufacturer's schedule.

When a tankless repair is not enough

Cracked heat exchangers, failed control boards on older units and chronic scale damage can make replacement the better call. When that happens, we explain the repair and replacement numbers side by side. Many owners replace a failed tankless with the same type. Others return to a tank, which is covered on our water heater replacement page.

Pricing a tankless job

Gas line size, vent length, the unit itself and wall or roof penetrations drive the cost. Descaling is a much smaller job. We state the price before we start and do not add work without asking.

Gas tankless heaters are certified to ANSI Z21.10.3, and the manufacturer's venting and gas line guidelines are part of that listing. We follow those guidelines and the local code, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC) or the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and we do not improvise a vent.
