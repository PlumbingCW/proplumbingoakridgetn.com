Planned water heater installation

Water Heater Installation in Oak Ridge, TN

A planned installation lets you choose the unit, the location and the connections without a flooded floor forcing the decision. We size the heater to your household, then set it, vent it and test it.

Why plan the install instead of waiting for failure

The worst time to shop for a water heater is when the old one is leaking into the garage. A planned installation in Oak Ridge, TN lets you compare fuel types, check the venting and pick the day. If the old heater is already leaking, our emergency replacement page covers that case.

Planned work also catches problems that rushed jobs skip, such as a corroded shutoff valve, a missing expansion tank or a drain pan that no longer drains. Fixing those at install time costs less than finding them after a leak.

Sizing the heater to the household

Tank size matters less than how fast the unit recovers and how many fixtures run at once. A family with teenagers who shower back to back needs a different unit than two adults. We ask about your peak morning routine, not just the number of people.

Gas, electric and heat pump: what changes at install

Gas heaters need a vent, combustion air and a properly sized gas line with a shutoff and a drip leg. Electric heaters need the right breaker and wire gauge for the elements. Heat pump heaters need air space around the unit and a place for condensate to drain.

In older Oak Ridge homes, the utility closet may never have been built for any of that. We measure clearances and check the vent path before we order the unit, so the install day goes smoothly.

The installation day, start to finish

  1. We confirm the unit and review the location with you.
  2. The plumber shuts off water, drains the old tank and removes it.
  3. New supply connectors, shutoff valves and the relief valve with a discharge line go in.
  4. Gas or electric connections are made and tested.
  5. We fill the tank, purge air, light or power the unit and check for leaks.
  6. You get a walkthrough of the thermostat and the maintenance schedule.

Extras worth considering at install

  • An expansion tank, if your system has a closed loop or a pressure regulator.
  • A drain pan with a line to a safe drain.
  • A water leak sensor beside the tank.
  • A mixing valve to cap hot water temperature at the taps.

Permits and rules

Permit and inspection rules vary by property and jurisdiction. Ask the City of Oak Ridge building office, or the office for your town if you live outside the city, whether your install needs one. We follow what they say, and we install to the manufacturer's instructions.

If you are comparing contractors, you can check any plumber's license on the State of Tennessee's contractor license search at search.cloud.commerce.tn.gov or by calling 1-800-544-7693.

What the price depends on

Price follows the unit, the fuel, the venting, the access and any code upgrades. We quote the whole job so there is no surprise at the end, and we tell you when an optional upgrade is truly optional.

Standards matter on install day. Gas storage water heaters are certified to ANSI Z21.10.1, venting follows the manufacturer's instructions and the local plumbing code, based on the IPC or the UPC, and electric connections follow the National Electrical Code (NEC), NFPA 70, as your jurisdiction has adopted it.
