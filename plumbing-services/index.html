How to use this page

Plumbing problems rarely arrive labeled. A wet ceiling could be a burst supply pipe, a leaking drain or a failed water heater upstairs. The list below groups our services by what they fix, and each page explains the signs, the usual causes and what a visit looks like.

If something is flooding, stop reading and call. The dispatcher will help you shut off the water first. For everything else, the groups below make it easy to find the right page.

Urgent or can it wait?

A sensible rule helps. If water is flowing where it should not, if sewage is coming up a drain or if a gas smell is present, treat it as urgent. A slow drain, a dripping faucet or a toilet that runs can wait for a daytime visit, though a quick call costs nothing.

Planned work gets a calmer process. We look at the house, explain the choices and give you the price before anything starts. That includes installs, upgrades and repipes.

What every visit includes

  • A plumber who explains what is wrong in plain words.
  • The options, with the price for each, before work begins.
  • A test of the finished work and a short walkthrough.
  • A stop to talk with you if the job changes once a wall is open.
